## How the fonts are loaded, and offline proof

Self-hosted `@font-face` in new `apps/web/src/app/fonts.css`, imported from `globals.css` (`tokens.css` itself untouched — generated, checked byte-for-byte). Files vendored under `apps/web/public/fonts/{archivo,be-vietnam-pro}/*.woff2`, each family with its `OFL.txt` alongside. `font-display: swap` (not V6's `block`) so first paint is never invisible text — the fallback is legible immediately and swaps in. No `next/font/google` (that fetches at build time); no network call anywhere in build, test, or runtime — every asset is a committed file. `apps/web/src/app/fonts.test.ts` proves this with a TDD pair: it fails before `globals.css` imports `fonts.css` (confirmed red), passes after (confirmed green), and asserts every `url()` in `fonts.css` resolves to a vendored file.

## C2 — empirical finding (not assumed)

Measured in-browser via canvas `measureText` own-vs-fallback comparison (`apps/web/e2e/typography.spec.ts`), against the real loaded font, not a hypothetical:

- **Archivo's `·` (U+00B7) measures 15.984375px — identical to forced Arial fallback (delta 0px).** Archivo still does not contain this glyph.
- Control: Archivo's `A` differs from Arial's `A` by >1px, proving the measurement technique actually detects a real font when one is active (so the 0px delta above isn't a broken test).
- Be Vietnam Pro's own `·` measures 12.24px, delta 3.74px from Arial — it does have its own glyph, as V6 found.
- Screenshot: `docs/research/assets/typography-c2-middot-comparison.png` (read visually — the Archivo and Arial dots look identical in weight/size; the Be Vietnam Pro dot is visibly different).

**C2 is confirmed, now proven rather than assumed.** The guard remains necessary.

## C1 — real metrics, not fallback metrics

Reproduced V6's worst-case Vietnamese diacritic-collision test with canvas `TextMetrics` ink bounds against the real self-hosted Be Vietnam Pro file: negative control at line-height 1.0 gives a **-1px** gap (overlap, same direction as V6's -4.00px), while the token floor of **1.3 gives +3.5px** (no collision). C1 holds against real metrics. Screenshot: `docs/research/assets/typography-c1-diacritic-stack.png` (visually clean separation, no overlapping ink).
